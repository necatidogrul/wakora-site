# Wakora — the unskippable alarm for heavy sleepers (iPhone)

> Wakora is an iPhone alarm clock that cannot be silenced with a single tap. To dismiss the
> alarm you must complete a wake-up mission: solve arithmetic, shake the phone a set number of
> times, walk steps, do push-ups, match a photo, or scan a barcode you left in another room.
> Free to download, with an optional Premium subscription. Previously published as WakeGuard;
> renamed to Wakora in August 2026.

## How it works

The alarm rings at full volume through Silent Mode and Focus because Wakora schedules a
system-level alarm and holds protected background audio. Dismissal is gated behind a mission you
choose in advance. Snoozing is possible but each snooze costs more than the last, with a daily
cap. Two minutes after dismissal Wakora asks whether you are still up, so going back to bed is
caught rather than silently rewarded.

## Frequently asked

**How is it different from a normal alarm?** Normal alarms die with one sleepy tap. Wakora
demands a real challenge — math, shaking or steps — before it goes quiet.

**Does it work on silent or Focus?** Yes. It uses a system-level alarm plus protected background
audio, so it rings at full volume regardless of Silent Mode or Focus.

**What if I force-quit the app?** A layered notification fallback fires even if iOS terminates
the app, and a backup alarm is scheduled a few minutes after the main one.

**Is Wakora the same app as WakeGuard?** Yes. It was published as WakeGuard until August 2026.
Same app, same developer; subscriptions and streaks carry over.

**Can I still snooze?** Yes, but each snooze costs more than the last, with a daily cap.
