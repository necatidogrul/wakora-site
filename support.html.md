# Wakora Support

Wakora is the alarm clock with real stakes: complete a wake-up challenge to dismiss, or — if you opted into Wager Mode — spend prepaid wallet credit to snooze.

## Frequently asked questions

My alarm didn't ring loudly. What should I check?

  Keep Wakora open or in the background overnight — do not force-quit (swipe away) the app.
  - Allow notifications in Settings → Notifications → Wakora.
  - Turn the device volume up; the alarm rings even in Silent Mode while the app is running in the background.

What is Wager Mode?
An optional commitment feature. You prepay wallet credit; each snooze spends some of it (starting at 50¢ and doubling). Credits never expire and are only spent when you confirm a snooze. If you leave Wager Mode off, there is no snooze button and nothing to spend.

Can I get wallet credit back as money?
Wallet credits are in-app credits with no cash value; they cannot be withdrawn. For App Store purchase refunds, use Apple's [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Wakora Premium → Cancel. Or inside the app: Settings → Manage Subscription.
